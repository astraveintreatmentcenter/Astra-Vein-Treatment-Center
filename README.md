<p dir="auto">Astra Vein Treatment Center 4209A Ave U, Brooklyn, NY 11234 718-222-0225 Astra Vein Treatment Center is a Brooklyn <a href="https://www.astraveinvascular.com/">veins treatment center</a> providing diagnosis and minimally invasive treatment for varicose veins, spider veins, venous insufficiency, leg swelling, leg heaviness, circulation problems, venous stasis ulcers, foot veins, hand veins, and other abnormal vein conditions. The <a href="https://www.google.com/maps?cid=8628780741087091931">vein center</a> also provides same-day surgical care and is part of a vein and fibroid clinic approved as an Ambulatory Surgery Center by The Joint Commission.</p>
<p dir="auto"></p>
<p dir="auto">The practice is led by George Bolotin, MD, Medical Director of Astra Vein Treatment Center and a double board-certified Diagnostic and Interventional Radiologist. Dr. Bolotin diagnoses and treats abnormal vein conditions, including varicose veins, spider veins, deep vein thrombosis, circulation problems, and related vascular concerns. His background in diagnostic radiology, interventional radiology, and vascular-focused training supports precise evaluation and image-guided treatment planning.</p>
<p dir="auto"></p>
<p dir="auto">Astra Vein Treatment Center focuses on identifying the root cause of symptoms, not only visible vein changes. Patients may seek care for bulging veins, spider veins, aching legs, swelling, heaviness, fatigue, skin changes, leg sores, discomfort after standing, or concerns related to poor circulation. Treatment planning may include diagnostic evaluation and minimally invasive outpatient procedures designed to relieve symptoms, improve circulation, reduce swelling, and support long-term vascular health.</p>
